# Account Safety

Account safety is an operational discipline rather than a guarantee. The following controls are designed to reduce risk and support safer multi-account workflows.

## Controls

- Keep a consistent session environment for each account.
- Apply a stable proxy policy and document changes.
- Use account- and action-level rate limiting.
- Add jitter to scheduled jobs where appropriate.
- Avoid repetitive, identical behavior across accounts.
- Require human review for sensitive replies, direct messages, deletions, and reports.
- Monitor account health, errors, and unusual response patterns.
- Pause an account automatically after repeated failures.

These practices can help reduce operational signals and the risk of cross-account mistakes. They do not guarantee immunity from restrictions, shadowbans, or enforcement.
